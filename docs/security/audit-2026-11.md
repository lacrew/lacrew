# Internal smart contract audit, November 2026

This is the report of LaCrew's **internal** audit of the onchain contracts. It is not a third-party audit. Nobody outside the project has reviewed this code yet. A formal external audit is planned before any mainnet deployment, and until it is published every deployment should be treated as test-only (see [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md)).

Status below is checked against `main` on 2026-11-26 (after PR #648 and the merges that followed, up to #674), not taken from PR descriptions alone.

## Scope and method

- **Scope:** the contracts under `contracts/src`: `OrgRegistry`, `Treasury`, `EscalationRouter`, `GovernanceModule`, `SessionRegistry`, `EpochStreamer`, `MarketplacePayments` and the policy modules in `contracts/src/policies`.
- **Out of scope:** the off-chain orchestrator, the SDK, the hosted cloud, and any deployment script or configuration.
- **Method:** manual review of the contracts, with a Foundry proof of concept (PoC) written for each finding that could be exploited. Slither runs in CI (`fail-on: high`) and a weekly Mythril scan runs ([#630](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/630)).
- **Regression tests:** the PoCs and invariants live in `contracts/test/AuditPoC.t.sol` and `contracts/test/AuditInvariant.t.sol` (merged in [#633](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/633)). Each states the fixed behaviour: the attack reverts with a specific error, or the invariant holds.
- **Severity** is our own judgement of impact on funds and on the org's authority model, assuming a deployment with real value: High (funds or control can be taken without human approval), Medium (limits can be bypassed or griefed, or a safety lever is missing), Low (hardening).

## Fixed

### LC-01: Low-tier governance executes while outvoted (High)

- **Where:** `GovernanceModule.execute`, `propose`
- **Problem:** A low-tier proposal executed once `yesVotes >= effectiveQuorumYes`, even in the block it was created, and the proposer chose the tier. Two agent seats could outvote the root, in one block, and remove a human, add a treasury spender or rewire a policy.
- **Fix:** Default tier is High. Low tier needs an allow-listed selector (only `addNode` at start; a `HumanRoot` hire is refused), waits `lowTierDelay` (1 day by default) unless every human seat voted yes, and every execute requires yes > no. Proposers must hold voting power.
- **Fix PR:** [#628](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/628). Regression: `AuditPoC.test_lowTierAgentsFireHumanInstantly`, `test_lowTierCannotHireHumanRoot`, `test_lowTierExecutesWhileOutvoted`, and `GovernanceTierInvariant`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-02: EpochStreamer pays any number of epochs in one tx (High)

- **Where:** `EpochStreamer.runEpoch`
- **Problem:** An operator could pass any epoch id and book many payrolls in one transaction, draining treasury liquidity ahead of schedule.
- **Fix:** Epochs are sequential and time-gated (epoch n unlocks at `epochStart + (n-1) * epochDuration`); the arbitrary-id `runEpoch` is removed.
- **Fix PR:** [#625](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/625). Regression: `AuditPoC.test_streamerOperatorDrainsLiquidityInOneTx` (`EpochNotReady`).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-03: A failed target call counted as a completed action (High)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter._finalizeAction`
- **Problem:** When the target call reverted (for example a purchase whose price moved), the router still spent the allowance, used a rate slot and marked the intent approved. Funds could be stranded in the target.
- **Fix:** A failed target call now reverts the whole `propose` or `resolve`. Nothing moves, no allowance, rate count or budget is consumed, and an escalated intent stays pending. Pull settlement ([#629](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/629)) was the first step.
- **Fix PR:** [#640](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/640). Regression: `AuditPoC.test_failedPurchaseStrandsFundsThenStolen`, `TreasuryRouterInvariant.invariant_failedCallStrandsNothing`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-04: `purchaseFor` settles from a shared token pool (High)

- **Where:** `MarketplacePayments.purchaseFor`
- **Problem:** Delivery was computed as `balanceOf - priorOwed`, a pool shared by every caller. An agent could propose `value = 0` and have a purchase funded by tokens stranded by another agent.
- **Fix:** The router reports the payment of the action in flight (`ISettlementContext`), and `purchaseFor` requires it to equal the price exactly (`DeliveryMismatch` otherwise).
- **Fix PR:** [#626](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/626). Regression: `MarketplaceRouterSettlement.t.sol`, `MarketplaceReceiptInvariant`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged. Behaviour change: an overpayment now reverts instead of being refunded.

### LC-05: Session issuer can mint a key for the root (High)

- **Where:** `SessionRegistry._issue`
- **Problem:** The delegated issuer could mint a session key for the root or a peer `HumanRoot`. The router then accepted that key as the human, and for the root an ALLOW spent its unlimited allowance with no approver above.
- **Fix:** Unless the caller is the root, the agent must be an active manager or worker node (`IssuerAgentNotEligible`). The issuer can only revoke sessions it issued and cannot supersede a root-issued one.
- **Fix PR:** [#634](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/634).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged. Breaking constructor change, redeploy needed.

### LC-06: Re-entrancy and rate count recorded after the call (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter.propose`, `resolve`
- **Problem:** The rate window was recorded after the target call, so a re-entering target saw the window without the in-flight action and nested proposals bypassed `maxActions`.
- **Fix:** The rate is counted before the external call and `propose` and `resolve` are `nonReentrant`.
- **Fix PR:** [#623](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/623). Test: `RouterReentrancy.t.sol`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-07: One agent can burn another agent's rate window (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter._requireUnprotectedTarget`
- **Problem:** Only the acting agent's own recorder was protected. Agent A could have the router call `rateRecorderOf[B].record(B)`, which trusts the router, and push B into ESCALATE.
- **Fix:** Every recorder bound in any slot (global or per-node, rate or value) is a protected target (`isBoundRecorder`, reference counted).
- **Fix PR:** [#652](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/652). Regression: `AuditPoC.test_crossAgentRateRecorderGrief`, `PerNodeRateRecorder.t.sol`. This test was red when [#633](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/633) merged and passes on current `main`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-08: Unbounded loops can exceed the block gas limit (Medium)

- **Where:** `OrgRegistry` (children, depth), `EpochStreamer` (recipients)
- **Problem:** Fan-out and tree depth were unbounded, so a large org could make `removeNode` or `runNextEpoch` too expensive to execute.
- **Fix:** `MAX_CHILDREN = 100`, `MAX_DEPTH = 32`, `MAX_RECIPIENTS = 100`.
- **Fix PR:** [#624](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/624).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged. Reparenting could deepen a subtree past the cap; see LC-22 (fixed).

### LC-09: Push settlement and non-vanilla tokens (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter`, `Treasury`, `MarketplacePayments`
- **Problem:** The router pushed tokens to the target before calling it, so tokens sent to a contract that did not account for them were lost. Treasury and the marketplace assumed a vanilla ERC-20, so a fee-on-transfer token left reserves unbacked.
- **Fix:** Calls with value are pull-based (approve exactly `value`, call, revoke, refund the unpulled rest). Transfers are measured and revert with `TransferAmountMismatch`. The treasury token is a protected target. `Treasury.shortfall()` exposes balance lost outside a transfer. Rebasing tokens are documented as unsupported (SPEC §4.2).
- **Fix PR:** [#629](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/629).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged. This closes the approve-and-call settlement mode (approve / call / reset on `EscalationRouter`).

### LC-10: No recovery from a lost or compromised root key (Medium)

- **Where:** `OrgRegistry`, `SessionRegistry`, `GovernanceModule`
- **Problem:** The root was fixed at deploy. A lost key had no recovery short of a redeploy, and `SessionRegistry` had no governor to act on a compromised issuer.
- **Fix:** A High-tier `GovernanceModule.rotateRoot(newRoot, rooted[])` rotates the root atomically across the contracts. `SessionRegistry` gets a governor. See LC-20 for the residual limit.
- **Fix PR:** [#627](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/627).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-11: No way to claw back an allowance or withdraw (Medium)

- **Where:** `Treasury`
- **Problem:** After firing an agent or spotting a bad grant, governance could not reduce an allowance or take funds back out.
- **Fix:** `Treasury.revokeAllowance(node, amount)` and `Treasury.withdraw(to, amount)` are governor-only, and `withdraw` proposals must be High tier (`WithdrawNotHighTier`).
- **Fix PR:** [#631](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/631).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged. Clearing the allowance automatically inside `removeNode` is not done: a fire is followed by a `revokeAllowance` proposal.

### LC-12: No emergency stop (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter`, `Treasury`
- **Problem:** There was no lever to halt spending quickly while an incident was investigated.
- **Fix:** `pause()` can be called by any active human seat or the current authority. `unpause()` is governor-only. While paused, settlement and allowance spending revert; rejection and escalation stay open.
- **Fix PR:** [#632](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/632). Test: `EmergencyPause.t.sol`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-13: Stale intents and state of a removed node survive (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter`, `SessionRegistry`, `Treasury`, `OrgRegistry`
- **Problem:** Intents never expired, and an address that was removed and added again inherited its old sessions and allowance.
- **Fix:** `OrgRegistry.generationOf` counts incarnations. Intents expire (`intentTtl`, 7 days by default) and `closeExpired` lets anyone reject them. Sessions, approvers and allowances from an earlier generation stop validating.
- **Fix PR:** [#649](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/649). Test: `IntentExpiry.t.sol`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-14: Transaction splitting under a per-call cap (Medium)

- **Where:** `SpendCapPolicy`, `EscalationRouter`
- **Problem:** A per-call cap admits any number of calls just under it, so a large spend split into small ones settled with no human.
- **Fix:** `CumulativeValuePolicy` budgets settled value per agent and per payee over a window, fed by the router's value recorder.
- **Fix PR:** [#621](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/621).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged for the router path: approve / call / reset on `EscalationRouter` and `CumulativeValuePolicy` are on `main`. A cumulative `maxTotalValue` on SESSIONS is **not** done and is not covered by this fix. Windows are anchored, so up to twice the budget can settle across a window boundary. This is documented.

### LC-18: Listing id squatting in `registerListing` (Low)

- **Where:** `MarketplacePayments.registerListing`
- **Problem:** The first caller to register a `listingId` became its seller permanently, so anyone could claim an id another party intended to use.
- **Fix:** The listing id is derived from the seller and a catalog id (`listingIdFor(seller, catalogId)`), so ids cannot collide across sellers.
- **Fix PR:** [#660](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/660).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-22: `OrgRegistry.reparent` could exceed `MAX_DEPTH` (Low)

- **Where:** `OrgRegistry.reparent`
- **Problem:** Moving a subtree under a deep parent could build a tree deeper than `MAX_DEPTH`. Only the root or the governor can reparent, and no funds are at risk, but deep trees defeat the LC-08 gas bound.
- **Fix:** `reparent` reverts (`MaxDepthExceeded`) if the deepest seat of the moved subtree would sit past `MAX_DEPTH`.
- **Fix PR:** [#664](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/664). Test: `OrgRegistryDepth.t.sol`.
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-15: Leftover bootstrap admin privileges on policy modules (Medium)

- **Where:** `WhitelistPolicy.admin`, `SpendCapPolicy.admin`, `EpochStreamer.operator`, `MarketplacePayments`
- **Problem:** The deployer stays `admin` of both policies after the governor is bound and can still change the whitelist and caps without a proposal. Constitutional control is therefore not complete.
- **Fix:** The admin is dropped when the governor binds. The governor can replace the `EpochStreamer` operator, and `MarketplacePayments` is handed to `GovernanceModule` in `deployOrg`.
- **Fix PR:** [#651](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/651), [#671](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/671).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-16: No session registry means anyone can propose as an agent (Medium)

- **Where:** `EscalationRouter.propose`, `_requireValidSession`
- **Problem:** While `sessionRegistry` is the zero address, `propose` does not check who `msg.sender` is, so any account can propose as any active agent. The setter allows the zero address.
- **Fix:** The router requires a wired `SessionRegistry` in `propose`.
- **Fix PR:** [#665](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/665).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-17: Vote weight is read at vote time (Medium)

- **Where:** `GovernanceModule.vote`
- **Problem:** `votingPower` was read when the vote was cast, not when the proposal was created. Weight changed after creation changed the outcome of a proposal already open.
- **Fix:** Seat weights are snapshotted at proposal creation. A seat revoked after creation keeps its snapshot weight on that proposal. A rotated-out root keeps its weight on open proposals, and the new root can only veto them.
- **Fix PR:** [#674](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/674).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

### LC-20: Outgoing root may veto its own rotation (Medium)

- **Where:** `GovernanceModule.rotateRoot`, veto semantics
- **Problem:** A root key that is compromised and actively used can veto its own rotation while it still holds its seat, because a veto stops an action but does not recover from it.
- **Fix:** The outgoing root loses its veto on its own rotation only when at least one other Human seat voted for the proposal. An org with a single Human seat keeps the old rule, since there is no one else to vouch for the rotation. The residual limit was first recorded in [#627](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/627).
- **Fix PR:** [#673](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/673).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

## Open

### LC-19: Governor hand-over is one step (Low)

- **Where:** `setGovernor` on the governed contracts
- **Problem:** Governance is handed to an address in one call. A typo or a contract that cannot act locks governance out. `GovernanceModule` forces High tier for the call, which limits who can do it but does not make it recoverable.
- **Fix:** Two-step hand-over: `setGovernor` names a `pendingGovernor`, and `acceptGovernor` completes it.
- **Fix PR:** [#677](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/677).
- **Status:** Fixed and merged.

## Withdrawn

### LC-21: Remove the unused `Treasury.spendAllowance(node, amount)` overload (Low)

- **Where:** `Treasury.sol:170`
- **Original claim:** The two-argument overload is unused and could be removed.
- **Why withdrawn:** Since pull settlement ([#629](https://github.com/lacrew/lacrew/pull/629)) the router calls it (`EscalationRouter.sol:484`). Removing it would break call settlement.
- **Status:** Withdrawn. No change.

## What this audit does not cover

- It is an internal review by the authors of the code. Authors miss things in their own work.
- The off-chain orchestrator, the SDK and the hosted cloud are out of scope.
- Fee-on-transfer and rebasing tokens, and issuer blocklists on the settlement asset, are handled by assumption (SPEC §4.2), not by proof.
- A formal external audit is planned before mainnet. This report will be superseded by it, and the findings above will be handed to the auditors.
